# Project Paper — Title Options

This document proposes candidate titles for the project paper describing the
**social-media "Smart Layer"** — a multilingual (Bangla / English / **Banglish**),
multimodal AI microservice that ingests social posts with their comment threads and
returns structured analytical JSON, using a **hybrid NLP → selective-LLM routing
pipeline** behind a pluggable (local vLLM ⇄ Groq) backend.

---

## Recommended title

> ### A Cost-Efficient Hybrid NLP–LLM Smart Layer for Multilingual, Multimodal Social-Media Analysis in Bangla, English, and Banglish

**Why:** it names the three things that make the work distinctive — the **hybrid
NLP→LLM routing** (the cost-control core), the **multimodal** (text + image)
analysis, and the **Bangla/English/Banglish** multilingual focus — while staying
readable. Good fit for a thesis/defense title page.

---

## Five candidate titles

1. **A Cost-Efficient Hybrid NLP–LLM Smart Layer for Multilingual, Multimodal
   Social-Media Analysis in Bangla, English, and Banglish**
   *(recommended — full-scope, defense-ready)*

2. **The Thinking Layer: A Confidence-Gated Hybrid Pipeline for Scalable
   Bangla–English–Banglish Social-Media Understanding**
   *(emphasizes the smart routing/triage idea and horizontal scale)*

3. **From Posts to Structured Insight: A Multimodal Microservice for Sentiment,
   Summarization, and Comment-Thread Analysis of Code-Mixed Social Media**
   *(emphasizes the input→structured-JSON contract and the post+thread unit)*

4. **Selective Intelligence at Scale: Routing Cheap NLP and Large Language Models
   for Multilingual Social-Media Analytics**
   *(emphasizes the "LLM as a scalpel, not a firehose" cost philosophy)*

5. **Designing a Production-Grade Multilingual Social-Media Analysis Microservice:
   A Hybrid NLP–LLM Architecture for Bangla, English, and Banglish**
   *(emphasizes the systems/architecture and production-engineering contribution)*
